Frequently asked questions

Everything brands need to know about getting started, running campaigns and working with verified creators on Opulent.

01 What is Opulent Influencers House?

Opulent Influencers House is a self-service influencer marketing platform that connects brands across UAE and GCC with verified creators. It gives brand teams direct access to talent, structured campaign tools and real-time performance data — without relying on agencies or intermediaries.

02 How do I create a brand account?

Visit the Sign Up page and select "I'm a Brand". Complete the registration form with your work email and password, verify your account via OTP, then fill in your company details. Once submitted, your brand profile will be reviewed and activated.

03 How does creator discovery work?

Brands can search and filter creators by niche, audience demographics, platform, engagement rate and market relevance. Every creator on the platform has been verified, so you can shortlist talent with confidence before reaching out directly.

04 What does "verified creator" mean?

Verified creators have passed identity checks and audience quality reviews on the platform. This reduces the risk of fake followers, misrepresented reach or brand safety issues — giving your team a more reliable pool of talent to work with.

05 How do I launch a campaign?

From your brand dashboard, create a new campaign by defining your objective, target audience, deliverables and timeline. You can then invite specific creators or open the campaign to applications. All briefs, approvals and communication happen inside the platform.

06 Can I communicate directly with creators?

Yes. The platform is built for direct brand-to-creator communication. You can negotiate terms, share briefs, request revisions and finalise deliverables without going through a third party. This speeds up approvals and keeps everything in one place.

07 What performance data is available?

Brands have access to real-time campaign analytics including views, engagement rates, reach, conversions and ROI tracking. Reports are designed to support fast decision-making so you can optimise active campaigns or scale what is working.

08 Which markets does the platform cover?

Opulent is built for brands operating across UAE and the wider GCC region, covering six markets. Creators on the platform have audiences relevant to these markets, and the infrastructure supports regional campaign execution and compliance requirements.

09 What subscription plans are available for brands?

Brands can choose from Starter, Growth and Elite plans depending on campaign volume and team size. Plans differ in the number of monthly creator collaborations, analytics depth and support level. Visit the pricing section on the homepage for current plan details.

10 How do I get support or book a strategy call?

You can reach the team via the contact page or by emailing info@ulegendarydigital.com. For a dedicated strategy session, use the "Book strategy call" button on the For Brands page. The team typically responds within 24 hours.
