Frequently asked questions

Everything brands need to know about collaborating with influencers, running campaigns and getting results on Opulent.

01 How does your platform help brands collaborate with influencers?

Our platform connects brands with relevant influencers based on niche, audience demographics, engagement, and campaign goals. Brands can discover creators, manage collaborations, track campaign performance, and streamline communication in one place.

02 How do I find the right influencers for my brand?

You can search influencers by category, location, audience size, engagement rate, and content style. This helps brands find creators whose audience aligns with their target market and campaign objectives.

03 What types of campaigns can brands run?

Brands can run various campaigns including product promotions, sponsored posts, giveaways, brand awareness campaigns, product launches, affiliate partnerships, and long-term ambassador programs.

04 How much does it cost to work with influencers?

Campaign costs vary depending on the influencer's audience size, engagement rate, niche, content type, and campaign requirements. Brands can choose collaborations that fit their budget and goals.

05 Can I track the performance of my influencer campaigns?

Yes. Brands can monitor campaign performance through analytics such as reach, impressions, engagement, clicks, conversions, and overall ROI to measure campaign success.

06 How do payments work on the platform?

Payments are handled securely through the platform. Brands can review deliverables before releasing payments to ensure transparency and trust throughout the collaboration process.

07 Can brands work with multiple influencers at the same time?

Absolutely. Brands can manage multiple influencer collaborations simultaneously, making it easier to scale campaigns and reach different audience segments.

08 What if an influencer does not deliver the agreed content?

Our platform provides collaboration guidelines and communication tools to help resolve issues quickly. Brands can also review agreements and campaign terms before starting collaborations.

09 Are influencer profiles verified?

We aim to maintain authentic creator profiles by reviewing influencer accounts, engagement quality, and audience authenticity to help brands make informed decisions.

10 Why should brands use influencer marketing?

Influencer marketing helps brands build trust, increase visibility, reach targeted audiences, and drive engagement through authentic recommendations from creators their audiences already follow and trust.
