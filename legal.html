Last updated June 2026 · CREOVA | Brand & Influencer

1. PURPOSE

These Community Guidelines and Content Policy establish the standards governing user conduct content creation and platform interaction within CREOVA | Brand Influencer.

The objective is to ensure a professional transparent and legally compliant ecosystem for creators’ brands agencies and all participants engaging in influencer marketing collaborations.

These guidelines are designed to:

  • Promote ethical and professional conduct
  • Ensure authenticity and trust in content
  • Prevent fraud manipulation and abuse
  • Protect intellectual property and user rights
  • Maintain marketplace integrity

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

These guidelines apply to all users and all platform activities including:

  • Creator profiles and portfolios
  • Campaign participation and deliverables
  • Communication between users
  • Content creation publishing and distribution
  • Financial and transactional interactions

All users are required to comply with these guidelines at all times.

3. ACCEPTABLE USER CONDUCT

Users must maintain a high standard of professionalism and integrity.

Acceptable conduct includes:

  • Honest representation of identity capabilities and services
  • Respectful and professional communication
  • Responsible participation in campaigns
  • Compliance with applicable laws and advertising regulations
  • Timely and transparent collaboration practices

4. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

All communication on the platform must remain relevant respectful and professional.

Users must not:

  • Use abusive offensive or threatening language
  • Engage in harassment or intimidation
  • Send spam or unsolicited promotional messages
  • Conduct off platform solicitation intended to bypass platform systems

Professional communication is essential for maintaining trust and collaboration quality.

5. CONTENT STANDARDS

All content must meet legal ethical and professional standards.

Content must:

  • Be original or used with proper authorization
  • Be accurate and not misleading
  • Comply with applicable advertising and consumer protection laws
  • Respect cultural sensitivities and public standards

Users are solely responsible for the content they publish.

6. PROHIBITED BEHAVIOR AND CONTENT

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Illegal or unlawful content
  • Fraudulent or deceptive practices
  • Hate speech discrimination or harassment
  • Content promoting violence or harmful activities
  • Explicit or restricted adult content were prohibited by law
  • Misleading health financial or investment claims
  • Defamation or false statements
  • Circumvention of platform systems or controls

Violations may result in enforcement actions as defined under the Platform Enforcement and Violation Policy.

7. FRAUD AND FAKE ENGAGEMENT

The platform enforces a strict zero tolerance policy toward artificial manipulation.

Prohibited activities include:

  • Purchasing fake followers or engagement
  • Use of bots or automated tools
  • Engagement exchange schemes or click farms
  • Artificial inflation of metrics
  • Manipulation of campaign performance data

Fraudulent practices also include:

  • Misrepresentation of audience analytics
  • Impersonation of individuals or brands
  • Creation of fake campaign listings
  • Payment manipulation attempts

All fraud related matters are further governed under the Fraud and Fake Engagement Policy.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY COMPLIANCE

Users must respect all intellectual property rights.

Users must not:

  • Upload copyrighted content without authorization
  • Use trademarks logos or brand assets without permission
  • Republish or reuse another user’s content without consent

Ownership licensing and rights are governed under the Intellectual Property Policy.

9. CREATOR RESPONSIBILITIES

Creators are responsible for maintaining authenticity professionalism and compliance.

Creators must:

  • Deliver agreed campaign outcomes
  • Maintain genuine audience engagement
  • Avoid misleading claims or exaggeration
  • Comply with advertising and disclosure laws
  • Align content with agreed brand messaging

10. BRAND RESPONSIBILITIES

Brands must uphold ethical and transparent marketing practices.

Brands must:

  • Provide clear campaign guidelines and deliverables
  • Avoid misleading or deceptive claims
  • Respect creator independence and professional integrity
  • Ensure compliance with applicable advertising regulations

Brands must not request or pressure creators to violate platform policies or laws.

11. ADVERTISING TRANSPARENCY

All promotional content must be clearly disclosed.

This includes:

  • Paid collaborations
  • Sponsored content
  • Affiliate partnerships
  • Gifted product endorsements

Disclosures must be visible clear and compliant with applicable laws and jurisdiction specific requirements.

12. MODERATION AND PLATFORM RIGHTS

The platform reserves the right to monitor review and moderate user activity and content.

Actions may include:

  • Removal of content
  • Restriction of visibility
  • Temporary or permanent account suspension
  • Escalation to legal or regulatory authorities where required

Moderation actions are taken to protect platform integrity user safety and regulatory compliance.

13. REPORTING VIOLATIONS

Users may report violations through the platform reporting mechanisms.

Reports should include:

  • Description of the issue
  • Relevant references or links
  • Supporting evidence where available

All reports will be reviewed in accordance with platform procedures.

14. ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

Violations of these guidelines may result in enforcement actions including:

  • Warning notices
  • Content removal
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent account termination
  • Restriction from campaigns or platform features
  • Legal escalation where applicable

Enforcement actions are governed by the Platform Enforcement and Violation Policy.

15. DATA MONITORING AND SECURITY

To ensure platform safety and compliance the platform may monitor user activity.

This may include:

  • Review of communications and interactions
  • Analysis of behavioral patterns
  • Detection of suspicious or fraudulent activity

Technical data processed may include:

  • Device information
  • Network identifiers
  • IP address
  • Geo location data

All data handling is governed by the Privacy Policy and Data Protection Policy and applicable laws.

16. POLICY UPDATES

These guidelines may be updated periodically to reflect:

  • Regulatory changes
  • Platform enhancements
  • Industry developments

Users will be notified of significant updates. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of revised policies.

17. REFERENCE AGREEMENTS

These guidelines must be read in conjunction with the following:

  • Payment Terms and Escrow Policy

Fraud & Fake Engagement Policy

Last updated June 2026 · CREOVA | Brand & Influencer

This Fraud and Fake Engagement Policy establishes the rules and enforcement mechanisms designed to protect the integrity of campaigns conducted through the CREOVA | Brand Influencer platform.

The platform is committed to maintaining a transparent and trustworthy ecosystem where brands and creators can collaborate based on authentic audience engagement and legitimate campaign performance.

This policy must be read together with the following agreements.

  • Terms of Use and Platform Agreement
  • Creator Agreement
  • Brand and Advertiser Agreement
  • Campaign Contract Framework
  • Payment Terms and Escrow Policy
  • Commission and Fee Policy
  • Refund and Dispute Resolution Policy
  • Privacy Policy and Data Protection Policy

By using the platform users agree to comply with the anti-fraud standards described in this policy.

1. PURPOSE OF THE POLICY

The purpose of this policy is to protect the credibility of the marketplace by preventing fraudulent behavior and artificial manipulation of engagement metrics.

Fraudulent activities can damage brand trust distort campaign performance and undermine the credibility of creators participating in the platform.

This policy establishes rules for identifying detecting and addressing fraudulent activity.

2. DEFINITION OF FAKE ENGAGEMENT

Fake engagement refers to artificially generated interactions designed to misrepresent the popularity or influence of a creator or campaign.

Examples of fake engagement may include:

  • Purchased followers
  • Bot generated likes comments or views
  • Artificial engagement generated through click farms
  • Automated social media interaction systems
  • Engagement pods designed to inflate performance metrics

Any activity intended to misrepresent the genuine reach or influence of a creator may be considered fraudulent.

3. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

The following activities are strictly prohibited on the platform.

  • Purchasing fake followers or engagement
  • Using automated bots to inflate interaction metrics
  • Participating in engagement exchange networks
  • Misrepresenting audience demographics or reach
  • Artificially inflating campaign performance reports

Users engaging in such activities may be subject to enforcement actions.

4. PLATFORM MONITORING AND DETECTION

The platform may implement monitoring systems designed to detect suspicious engagement patterns.

Monitoring mechanisms may include:

  • Engagement pattern analysis
  • Audience authenticity verification tools
  • Social media analytics systems
  • Automated fraud detection algorithms

The platform may also review campaign performance anomalies or unusual engagement spikes.

Technical monitoring may involve the collection of device information network data IP address information and geo location data in accordance with the Privacy Policy and Data Protection Policy.

5. AUDIENCE AUTHENTICITY VERIFICATION

Creators may be required to verify their audience authenticity during onboarding or campaign participation.

Verification procedures may include:

  • Social media account analysis
  • Engagement quality assessment
  • Follower growth pattern evaluation
  • Audience demographic verification

The platform may use third party analytics tools to assist with authenticity verification.

6. INVESTIGATION PROCEDURES

If suspicious activity is detected the platform may initiate an investigation.

Investigations may involve:

  • Reviewing engagement analytics
  • Requesting information from creators or brands
  • Assessing campaign performance reports
  • Reviewing historical activity on the platform

Users may be required to cooperate with investigations conducted by the platform.

7. ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

If fraudulent activity is confirmed the platform may take enforcement actions including:

  • Warning notices issued to the user
  • Temporary suspension of campaign participation
  • Removal from active campaigns
  • Permanent termination of platform accounts

The severity of enforcement actions may depend on the nature and scale of the fraudulent activity.

8. CAMPAIGN CONSEQUENCES

Campaigns affected by fraudulent engagement may be subject to corrective actions including:

  • Cancellation of the campaign
  • Adjustment of campaign payments
  • Refunds issued to brands where appropriate
  • Removal of fraudulent campaign data from analytics reports

The platform may also notify affected parties where campaign metrics are found to be artificially manipulated.

9. BRAND RESPONSIBILITIES

Brands participating in campaigns must also avoid engaging in fraudulent practices.

Prohibited brand activities may include:

  • Artificially boosting campaign engagement
  • Using external services to manipulate campaign metrics
  • Misrepresenting campaign results for marketing claims

Brands are expected to uphold transparency in campaign reporting.

10. CREATOR RESPONSIBILITIES

Creators participating in campaigns must maintain authentic audience engagement.

Creators are responsible for:

  • Ensuring followers are organically obtained
  • Avoiding engagement manipulation services
  • Providing accurate campaign performance information
  • Complying with advertising disclosure requirements

Creators may be required to provide access to campaign performance data when requested by the platform.

11. REPORTING FRAUDULENT ACTIVITY

Users may report suspected fraudulent activity through the platform reporting system.

Reports should include:

  • Description of suspicious behavior
  • Campaign identification details
  • Supporting evidence where available

The platform may review such reports and initiate investigations where appropriate.

12. RECORD RETENTION

The platform may maintain records of fraud investigations and enforcement actions for purposes including:

  • Platform security monitoring
  • Fraud prevention improvements
  • Compliance with legal obligations

Records may include technical data network information device identifiers and geo location data where necessary for fraud detection.

13. POLICY UPDATES

This Fraud and Fake Engagement Policy may be updated periodically to address new forms of digital fraud evolving platform technologies and regulatory requirements.

Users will be notified of significant policy updates.

Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Platform Enforcement & Violation Policy

Last updated June 2026 · CREOVA | Brand & Influencer

This Platform Enforcement and Violation Policy establish the framework for identifying investigating and enforcing actions against violations of platform rules across the CREOVA | Brand & Influencer platform.

This policy defines how the platform responds to breaches of its agreements policies and guidelines and outlines the consequences applicable to users who fail to comply with platform standards.

This policy is designed to operate as a central enforcement mechanism and must be read together with all other platform policies.

LINKED POLICIES AND CROSS REFERENCES

This policy applies to violations arising under the following documents.

  • Terms of Use and Platform Agreement
  • Creator Agreement
  • Brand and Advertiser Agreement
  • Campaign Contract Framework
  • Payment Terms and Escrow Policy
  • Commission and Fee Policy
  • Refund and Dispute Resolution Policy
  • Fraud and Fake Engagement Policy
  • KYC and User Verification Policy
  • Community Guidelines and Content Policy
  • Privacy Policy and Data Protection Policy

All violations under the above policies may trigger enforcement actions under this document.

1. PURPOSE OF ENFORCEMENT POLICY

The purpose of this policy is to ensure consistent fair and transparent enforcement of platform rules.

This policy aims to:

  • Protect the integrity of the platform ecosystem
  • Ensure compliance with all platform agreements
  • Provide a structured approach to handling violations
  • Define enforcement actions and penalties
  • Prevent misuse of platform services

This policy applies to all users including creators’ brands agencies and any third-party participants.

2. DEFINITION OF VIOLATION

A violation occurs when a user breaches any obligation restriction or requirement set out in the platform’s legal documents or policies.

Violations may include but are not limited to:

  • Breach of contractual obligations
  • Non-compliance with campaign requirements
  • Violation of community or content standards
  • Submission of false or misleading information
  • Attempts to bypass platform systems or controls

Violations may be identified through platform monitoring user reports or internal reviews.

3. CLASSIFICATION OF VIOLATIONS

Violations may be categorized based on severity and impact.

3.1 Minor Violations

Minor violations may include:

  • Unintentional non-compliance with minor platform requirements
  • Delayed response in campaign obligations
  • Minor content deviations without material impact

3.2 Moderate Violations

Moderate violations may include:

  • Repeated minor violations
  • Failure to meet campaign deliverables
  • Miscommunication affecting campaign outcomes
  • Breach of communication standards

3.3 Severe Violations

Severe violations may include:

  • Fraudulent activity as defined in the Fraud and Fake Engagement Policy
  • Submission of false identity or KYC information
  • Payment related misconduct or abuse
  • Intellectual property infringement
  • Abuse harassment or harmful conduct
  • Circumvention of platform payment systems

Severity classification will determine the level of enforcement action applied.

4. DETECTION AND REPORTING

Violations may be detected through:

  • Automated monitoring systems
  • Analytics and behavioral pattern analysis
  • Reports submitted by users
  • Internal audits and compliance checks

Users may report violations by providing:

  • Description of the issue
  • Relevant campaign or user details
  • Supporting evidence where available

The platform may initiate investigations based on such reports.

5. INVESTIGATION PROCESS

Upon detection of a potential violation the platform may initiate an investigation.

The investigation process may include:

  • Review of user activity and platform records
  • Analysis of communication between parties
  • Evaluation of campaign data and deliverables
  • Verification of identity or transaction information

Technical data including device information IP address network activity and geo location data may be used for investigation purposes in accordance with the Privacy Policy and Data Protection Policy.

Users are required to cooperate with investigations and provide requested information where necessary.

6. ENFORCEMENT ACTION FRAMEWORK

Based on the findings of an investigation the platform may take appropriate enforcement actions.

6.1 Warning Notices

Issued for minor violations.

  • Formal notification of non-compliance
  • Guidance on corrective actions

6.2 Temporary Restrictions

Applied for moderate violations.

  • Suspension from campaign participation
  • Limited access to platform features
  • Temporary account restrictions

6.3 Financial Actions

Where applicable.

  • Withholding of payments
  • Adjustment of campaign compensation
  • Reversal of transactions were justified

6.4 Account Suspension

Applied for serious or repeated violations.

  • Temporary deactivation of account access
  • Restriction from platform activities

6.5 Account Termination

Applied for severe violations.

  • Permanent removal from the platform
  • Cancellation of all active engagements
  • Restriction from re registration

Enforcement actions may be applied individually or in combination depending on the severity of the violation.

7. URGENT ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

The platform reserves the right to take immediate action without prior notice in situations involving:

  • Fraudulent activity
  • Security risks
  • Legal or regulatory compliance concerns
  • Threats to user safety or platform integrity

Such actions may include immediate suspension or restriction of accounts.

8. APPEALS AND REVIEW PROCESS

Users may request a review of enforcement actions taken against their account.

Appeal requests should include:

  • Explanation of the issue
  • Supporting evidence
  • Clarification of disputed findings

The platform may review the appeal and determine whether to:

  • Uphold the original decision
  • Modify enforcement actions
  • Reverse the decision where justified

Decisions made after appeal review may be considered final within the platform framework.

9. REPEAT VIOLATIONS

Users with repeated violations may be subject to escalated enforcement actions.

Repeated non-compliance may result in:

  • Increased severity of penalties
  • Extended suspension periods
  • Permanent account termination

The platform may maintain records of prior violations to assess repeat behavior.

10. CROSS POLICY ENFORCEMENT

This policy operates in conjunction with all other platform policies.

Examples include:

  • Fraud related violations handled in alignment with the Fraud and Fake Engagement Policy
  • Identity related violations aligned with the KYC and User Verification Policy
  • Content related violations aligned with the Community Guidelines and Content Policy
  • Payment disputes aligned with the Refund and Dispute Resolution Policy

This ensures consistency in enforcement across all areas of the platform.

11. RECORD RETENTION

The platform may maintain records of violations investigations and enforcement actions for purposes including:

  • Platform integrity monitoring
  • Fraud prevention
  • Legal compliance
  • Dispute resolution

Records may include technical data network identifiers device information IP address and geo location data where relevant to enforcement activities.

12. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

The platform’s enforcement actions are undertaken to maintain the integrity of the marketplace.

The platform shall not be liable for losses arising from enforcement actions taken in good faith in accordance with this policy and other platform agreements.

13. POLICY UPDATES

This Platform Enforcement and Violation Policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational requirements legal obligations and evolving platform risks.

Users will be notified of significant updates.

Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
