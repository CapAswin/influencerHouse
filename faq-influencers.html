Frequently asked questions

Everything creators need to know about joining the platform, getting discovered and collaborating with premium brands.

01 Who can join as a creator?

Opulent welcomes micro, mid-tier and top-tier creators, influencers, celebrities, public figures and sports personalities. The key requirements are authentic audience engagement, consistent content quality and a professional approach to brand collaboration.

02 How do I sign up as an influencer?

Go to the Sign Up page and select "I'm an Influencer". Enter your email and password, verify via OTP, then complete your profile with personal details, category, niche and media licence. Once submitted, your profile will be reviewed before activation.

03 What is the verification process?

Verification includes an identity check and an audience quality review. You will be asked to upload a media licence or relevant document during registration. Verified status increases your visibility to brands and signals credibility on the platform.

04 How do brands find and contact me?

Brands search the creator directory using filters like niche, audience size and market. A complete, well-optimised profile with accurate category and niche tags increases your chances of being shortlisted. Brands can then reach out to you directly through the platform.

05 Can I set my own rates?

Yes. Opulent gives creators full control over their pricing. You set your own rates, negotiate directly with brands and maintain complete transparency over your value. There are no hidden fees or forced rate structures imposed by the platform.

06 How do I manage my collaborations?

Your creator dashboard lets you track active campaigns, review briefs, submit deliverables and communicate with brands — all in one place. You can manage multiple collaborations simultaneously and keep a clear record of each partnership's status and timeline.

07 What kind of brands will I work with?

Opulent works with brands across luxury and lifestyle, real estate, hospitality, beauty, technology, fashion and more — primarily operating in UAE and GCC markets. The platform is designed for premium collaborations, so the brands you encounter are typically serious about quality and long-term partnerships.

08 Are there any fees to join as a creator?

Creator registration and profile setup are part of the platform's subscription model. Specific fee details depend on the plan tier. Visit the pricing section on the homepage or contact the team for the most up-to-date information on creator plan options.

09 Can I track my performance and growth?

Yes. The platform provides real-time analytics so you can monitor engagement, campaign performance and audience growth. These insights help you understand what is working, strengthen your profile and make a stronger case when negotiating with brands.

10 How do I get support or ask a question?

Reach the team via the contact page or email info@ulegendarydigital.com. The team typically responds within 24 hours and can assist with profile setup, verification questions and collaboration support.
