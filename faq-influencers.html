Frequently asked questions

Everything creators need to know about joining the platform, getting discovered and building long-term brand partnerships.

01 How can I join the platform as an influencer?

You can sign up by creating an influencer profile, adding your social media accounts, audience details, niche, and portfolio to start connecting with brands.

02 Do I need a minimum number of followers to join?

Not necessarily. Brands often look for engagement and audience relevance rather than just follower count. Micro and nano influencers can also secure collaborations.

03 How do brands find and contact influencers?

Brands can discover influencers through search filters such as niche, engagement, audience demographics, and content style. If selected, brands can contact influencers directly through the platform.

04 How do I get paid for collaborations?

Payments are processed securely through the platform after campaign requirements and deliverables are completed and approved.

05 Can I choose which brands to work with?

Yes. Influencers have full control over accepting or declining collaboration requests based on their interests, values, and audience fit.

06 What type of content can influencers create?

Influencers can create various types of content including Instagram posts, reels, stories, TikTok videos, YouTube content, blogs, reviews, unboxings, and live promotions.

07 How can I increase my chances of getting brand deals?

To improve your chances of securing brand collaborations, keep your profile updated, maintain strong engagement, post high-quality content consistently, and clearly showcase your niche, audience insights, and past collaborations. Influencers can also explore our premium plans, which offer additional features such as enhanced profile visibility, advanced analytics, and priority access to campaign opportunities to help creators grow faster and connect with more brands.

08 Are there any fees to join the platform?

Signing up as an influencer is free. We offer a free account that allows creators to create a profile, connect with brands, and explore collaboration opportunities. In addition, we also provide premium influencer plans with advanced features, enhanced visibility, campaign tools, analytics, and additional benefits designed to help influencers grow their presence and secure more brand partnerships. Pricing and features may vary depending on the selected plan.

09 What happens if a brand changes campaign requirements?

Influencers can communicate directly with the brand to discuss updates or adjustments before continuing the collaboration to ensure both parties are aligned.

10 Why should influencers join this platform?

The platform helps influencers connect with brands, grow their network, secure paid collaborations, manage campaigns efficiently, and build long-term partnership opportunities.
